How it works

How an order gets to you.

  1. 1

    Order and pay

    Pick what you need and pay with mobile money or card.

  2. 2

    The shop packs it

    The seller confirms your order and gets it ready.

  3. 3

    A rider picks it up

    A nearby rider accepts and heads to the shop. Follow them on the map.

  4. 4

    Hand-off by QR

    Show your QR code at the door so the right order reaches the right person.

  5. 5

    Everyone gets paid

    The shop and the rider see their earnings in the app.

Safe by design

Built so you can trust every order.

Payments through licensed providers

Your money moves through licensed mobile money and card providers. Pocket Shop never asks for your PIN.

Every hand-off confirmed

Riders scan your QR code at the door, so there's a record that the right person received the order.

Prices you can see

Item prices, the delivery fee and any service fee are shown before you pay. No surprises at the door.
